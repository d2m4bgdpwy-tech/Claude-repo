Price. Cut. Install.

That's the whole job, and we own all three parts of it. Below is exactly what happens at each stage, what it costs, and what we don't do.

Stage one

We price the job

Countertop quotes should be boring. Ours are: a line for material, a line for the edge, a line for each cutout, a line for tear-out, a line for install. You can see what every decision costs and change your mind before anything is ordered.

  • Free in-home measure anywhere in the Metroplex, usually within 48 hours of your call.
  • Written line-item quote the same day, good for 30 days.
  • Slab selection help — we'll meet you at the yard and pull the racks with you.
  • No change orders unless you change the scope. If we misread the job, that's on us.

Working from plans instead of finished cabinets? Send the drawings and we'll quote off those, then confirm at template.

Stage two

We cut the stone

Fabrication happens in our own shop in Garland — we don't farm it out. Your cabinets are laser-templated once they're set and level, and that digital template drives the saw, so what comes off the table matches your kitchen instead of matching a tape measure.

  • Laser templating accurate to about 1/16", including out-of-square walls.
  • CNC saw and waterjet for clean cutouts, tight radii and inside corners that don't crack.
  • Seam planning you approve — we lay the template digitally over photos of your actual slabs.
  • Every edge profile from a simple eased edge to a mitered 3" waterfall.
  • 3–5 working days in the shop for a typical kitchen.
Stage three

We install the stone

Install day is our crew, in our truck, with our name on it. They protect your floors on the way in, set and level the tops, bond and polish the seams on site, reconnect your sink and disposal, seal natural stone, and take the old tops with them when they go.

  • Tear-out and haul-off of laminate, tile or existing stone.
  • Plumbing disconnect and reconnect for standard sinks and disposals.
  • On-site seam work colored to your slab, then polished flush.
  • Support and shimming for overhangs, with steel brackets where the span calls for it.
  • Most kitchens finish in 4–6 hours, and you can use the counters the same evening.

We don't do electrical, gas lines, cabinet work or tile backsplash. When a job needs those, we'll tell you up front and hand you names of people we actually work with.

Warranty

What we stand behind

Lifetime on workmanship

Seams, overhang support, leveling and cutouts. If our work fails, we come back and fix it, however long it's been.

Manufacturer warranty

Quartz carries its brand's warranty — typically 10 to 25 years, and we register it for you on install day.

A premium impregnating sealer on natural stone, warranted against staining for 15 years. Standard sealing is always included free.
