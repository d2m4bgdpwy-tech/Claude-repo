Before you send it

What helps us price it fast.

What should I include with a bid request?

Whatever you have: architectural drawings, millwork shop drawings, a door and hardware schedule, an FF&E list, or photos of the space. If material has already been purchased, tell us who supplied it and when it lands.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within one business day. If the scope is large or out of state, we may call first to walk through the schedule before putting numbers on paper.

Do you work as a subcontractor to a general contractor?

Yes — most of our hospitality work is under a GC or a purchasing agent. We are also happy to contract directly with owners and property managers.

Do you travel out of state?

We do. Our crews have run full resort renovations away from home, including Kauai, Hawaii. Travel and per diem are spelled out in the bid so nothing is a surprise.
