Details people notice

Where the craftsmanship actually shows

Seams that disappear

Color-matched epoxy, tooled flush and polished on site. On patterned stone we align the veining across the joint before it ever gets bonded.

Book-matched slabs

Sequential slabs mirrored across a seam so the pattern opens like a butterfly — the detail that makes a waterfall island look intentional.

Overhangs that don't flex

Anything past 10 inches gets steel. We size the brackets to the span so you can sit at the bar without the top moving.

Polished cutouts

Undermount sink reveals are cut on the waterjet and hand-polished, so the exposed edge reads as finished stone, not a saw cut.

Matching splashes

4-inch splashes cut from your leftover material so the color and movement continue instead of clashing with a second slab.

Clean job sites

Floors protected, cuts made outside, shop vacuum on the way out. You get a kitchen back, not a project.
