What we do

Three things, done under one roof

The process

From first call to finished kitchen

  1. Measure & quote

    We come out, measure your cabinets and talk through material, edges and seams. You get a written quote the same day — usually within a couple of hours.

  2. Pick your slab

    Walk the racks with us at our suppliers' yards and tag the exact slabs that go in your home. On granite and quartzite you approve the layout before anything is cut.

  3. Template & fabricate

    Cabinets get laser-templated once they're set. Cutting, edging and polishing take three to five working days in our shop.

  4. Install day

    We protect your floors, set and seam the tops, reconnect the plumbing, seal the stone and clean up. Most kitchens take four to six hours.

Questions

The things people ask us first

How much do granite countertops cost in DFW?

Most kitchens land between $52 and $88 per square foot installed, depending on the material tier and edge profile. A typical 45–55 sq ft kitchen runs roughly $2,800–$4,800 all in, including templating, fabrication, cutouts and installation. Our instant estimator will put a real number on your specific job.

Granite or quartz — which should I pick?

Granite is natural stone: every slab is different, it handles heat without a flinch, and it needs sealing about once a year. Quartz is engineered, so the pattern is consistent slab to slab, it never needs sealing, and it resists staining better — but it can scorch under a hot pan. If you cook heavy, we lean granite. If you want a clean marble look with no maintenance, we lean quartz.

How long does the whole thing take?

Seven to ten days from template to install is typical. Fabrication itself takes three to five working days. If you're on a deadline, tell us — we run rush jobs when the shop schedule allows.

Do you remove the old countertops?

Yes. Tear-out and haul-off is a line item on the quote, and we handle disconnecting and reconnecting the sink plumbing and disposal too. We don't move gas lines or do electrical.

Will I be able to see the seams?

You'll be able to find them if you look, but we plan them so you don't. Seams go behind the sink, at the corner of an L, or where the pattern naturally breaks — never in the middle of an island if the slab lets us avoid it. We show you the seam plan before we cut.

Do you work with builders and contractors?

Every week. We handle multi-unit and spec work, hold a standing schedule for builders, and can work off your plans instead of a site measure when the cabinets aren't set yet. Ask about trade pricing.
