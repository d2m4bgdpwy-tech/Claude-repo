Service 01

Mill Work Installations

Casework, paneling, trim and feature walls installed by carpenters who know the difference between close enough and finished.

  • Guest-room and suite casework — vanities, credenzas, headboard walls, closets
  • Crown, base, chair rail, door and window casing
  • Wall paneling, slat walls and decorative feature walls
  • Reception desks, back bars, host stands and service counters
  • Countertop setting, scribing and seaming
  • Restroom partitions, lockers and accessories

We work in new construction and in buildings that never closed — phased floor by floor, protected, and cleaned up every shift.

Service 02

FF&E

Furniture, fixtures and equipment handled from the truck to the turnover — including everything you already purchased somewhere else.

  • Receiving, inventory and damage reporting
  • Warehousing, staging and floor distribution
  • Guest-room FF&E sets, including case goods and seating
  • Restaurant and bar fixtures, booths and dining room packages
  • Retail and market fixtures, slatwall and merchandising systems
  • Mirrors, art, accessories, dispensers and signage
  • Debris removal and old-product tear-out

No job is too big or small for Yakbuilt to complete, whether it involves new cabinets in a hotel or resort, or FF&E in a local restaurant.

Service 03

Doors & Hardware

Thousands of openings, hung and adjusted so they close the way the hardware schedule says they should.

  • Wood and hollow metal doors and frames
  • Hinges, closers, locksets, exit devices and coordinators
  • Electronic locks and access-control prep
  • Pocket doors, barn doors and sliding assemblies
  • Custom carved and specialty doors from our own shop
  • Fire-rated openings, labels and hardware verification
  • Adjustment, keying coordination and punch

On one resort renovation alone we installed more than 2,000 new doors and their hardware.

How a job runs

The same four steps, every project.

Clear scope, staged material, our own crew, and a punch list that actually gets closed.

Walk the job and price it

Drawings, cut sheets, hardware schedules and delivery dates reviewed, then a written number with scope, crew size and duration spelled out.

Build or receive the material

What we build, we build in Canton. What you bought, we receive, inventory and stage so the crew is never waiting on a box.

Install with our own crew

Crews travel to the property and work the schedule — phased around operations, clean at the end of every shift.

Punch it out and hand it over

Doors adjusted, hardware functioning, reveals consistent, surfaces protected. Exceptional work, all the way to the finish.

Need a number on a scope like this?

Send the drawings, the hardware schedule or just a photo of the space. We will tell you what it takes.
