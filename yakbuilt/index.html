How a job runs

Straightforward from walk-through to punch list.

The same four steps whether it is one restaurant dining room or an entire hotel tower.

Walk the job and price it

We review drawings, cut sheets and delivery schedules, walk the space where we can, and put a written number in front of you — scope, crew size and duration spelled out.

Build or receive the material

Doors, cabinets and casework get built in our workshop in Canton. Material you have already purchased gets received, inventoried and staged so nothing is missing the day we start.

Install with our own crew

Our crews travel to the job and work to the schedule — floor by floor, phase by phase, clean at the end of every shift so the space stays usable around us.

Punch it out and hand it over

Doors adjusted, hardware functioning, reveals consistent, finishes protected. We stay until the list is closed — exceptional work, all the way to the finish.

Common questions

Answers before you call.

Do you install millwork and fixtures we have already purchased?

Yes. A large share of our work is installing material that has already been bought — casework, doors, partitions, fixtures and FF&E packages. We receive it, inventory it, stage it and install it, and we tell you up front if something arrived damaged or short.

Can you build the cabinets and doors as well as install them?

We can. Doors, cabinets and casework are built in our own workshop in Canton, Texas, then delivered to the job ready to set. On a single project we have built and installed the cabinets for more than 300 guest rooms.

What size projects do you take?

No job is too big or small for Yakbuilt to complete, whether it involves new cabinets in a hotel or resort, or FF&E in a local restaurant.

Will you travel to our property?

Yes. We are based in East Texas and travel for hospitality and commercial work — including a full resort renovation in Kauai, Hawaii with over 2,000 doors and hardware.

Can you work in an occupied hotel or restaurant?

Most of our renovation work happens in buildings that are still open. We phase the work floor by floor or room by room, protect finished surfaces, and clean up at the end of every shift.

Send us the drawings. We’ll send you a number.

Tell us what the scope is and when it needs to be done. You will hear back from a person who has actually done the work.
