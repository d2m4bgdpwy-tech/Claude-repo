About us

We take pride in our follow-through.

Yakbuilt, LLC is a commercial contractor in Canton, Texas offering mill work installations, FF&E, and doors & hardware.

Who we are

From our workshop to your punch list.

From doors and cabinets built in our own workshop, to the installation of purchases already made, our goal is to provide exceptional work all the way to the finish.

No job is too big or small for Yakbuilt to complete, whether it involves new cabinets in a hotel or resort, or FF&E in a local restaurant. We have hung more than 2,000 doors on one property and built the cabinets for 300+ guest rooms on another — and we have also spent a week setting fixtures in a single dining room, because that is what the customer needed.

Most of what we do is finish work: the part of the job everyone sees, done at the end of the schedule, when there is the least amount of time left. That is exactly where follow-through matters.

Own crews We do not hand your finish work to whoever is available. Our people travel to the job and see it through.
Own shop Doors, cabinets and panels built in Canton, Texas — so a field problem gets solved instead of reordered.
Own word Honest pricing, honest schedules, and a punch list we stay on until it is closed.
